Copy Protect
============

Copy Protect is a digital content protection solution designed to help reduce unauthorized copying of protected media and files.

Supported Content
-----------------

Depending on the product configuration, protected content can include documents, PDFs, images, audio, and video.

Typical Uses
------------

* Protect educational content.
* Help content creators distribute protected media.
* Support business content distribution.
* Protect files shared through removable media.

The product is designed to provide a practical way to control how protected digital content is distributed and accessed.
